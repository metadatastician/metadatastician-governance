== Architecture decisions

Estate-wide architecture decision records live in `docs/decisions/`:

* link:docs/decisions/ADR-2026-STACK-MIGRATION.adoc[ADR-2026 — Stack migration] — deprecates Nix in favour of Guix and ReScript in favour of AffineScript.
* link:docs/decisions/ADR-2026-BUN-AFFINESCRIPT-TRANSITION.adoc[ADR-2026 — Bun and AffineScript transition] — deprecates TypeScript in favour of AffineScript and Deno/NPM in favour of Bun.

These were previously unlinked from this README, so they were effectively
undiscoverable despite being public.

Individual projects bind through the templates in `templates/`. Machine-readable templates live under `.machine_readable/templates/`. Security reports follow link:SECURITY.md[SECURITY.md]; contributions follow link:.github/CONTRIBUTING.adoc[CONTRIBUTING.adoc].
